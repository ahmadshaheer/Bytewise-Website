# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Mixed audience: procurement officers, executives, and technical leads at corporations, NGOs, and government institutions evaluating ICT partners. They arrive skeptical, often mid-vendor comparison, and need fast proof of competence.

## Product Purpose

Establish Bytewise Technologies as a credible, capable ICT partner for software development, ERP systems, IT equipment and infrastructure, and strategic IT consulting. Success means visitors trust the firm enough to initiate contact or advance a procurement conversation.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, modern, technically confident. Professional without startup flash. The site should feel like a firm that builds serious systems, not one selling templates.

Voice: direct, specific, and calm under scrutiny. Emphasize reliability, clarity, and practical delivery.

## Anti-references

Cheap template and stock-photo agency sites. Avoid generic layouts, placeholder aesthetics, and anything that reads as outsourced or unconsidered. No startup hype, buzzword stacking, or decorative visuals that substitute for substance.

## Design Principles

1. **Credibility before spectacle** — Every section earns trust through clarity and specificity, not animation for its own sake.
2. **Show competence, not hype** — Concrete services, projects, and outcomes over vague claims.
3. **Institutional readiness** — Tone and structure suitable for government and NGO procurement review.
4. **Sharp craft** — Precise typography, intentional spacing, no visual clutter.
5. **Evolve the neo direction** — Refine the existing arctic/navy/green system in the codebase; do not restart from a generic IT vendor template.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Basic best practices: semantic HTML, meaningful alt text, keyboard navigation, sufficient color contrast, and respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`. No formal WCAG certification target, but avoid obvious barriers for procurement reviewers and mixed technical literacy.
